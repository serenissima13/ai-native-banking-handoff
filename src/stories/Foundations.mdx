# Foundations

Mirrors Figma page **03 Foundations**. Every example below renders through the generated CSS
(`src/tokens/tokens.css` and `styles.css`) or reads straight from `tokens/figma-tokens.json` — nothing
on this page is a hand-typed colour, size or font value. Switch the **Theme** toolbar above to see
Light and Dark; nothing here duplicates a story to show both.

## Tokens: three tiers

**Primitives** → raw values with no meaning of their own (`color/ink/500`, `space/16`). **Semantic** →
what the value is *for*, in Light and Dark (`color/text/primary`, `space/md`) — most Semantic tokens
alias a Primitive. **Component** → one component's own names, aliasing Semantic (`parameter-chip/confirmed/bg`).
A chain from the ParameterChip itself: `parameter-chip/confirmed/bg` → `color/surface/brand-subtle` →
`color/ion/50` (Light) or `color/ion/950` (Dark). Nothing about Light or Dark is written in the
component's own CSS — it only ever reaches for the Component tier.

## Colour

### Primitives — Ink, Ion, Aura

Ink is the neutral ramp (text, surfaces, borders in both modes); Ion is the brand colour — action and your money;
Aura is the assistant's own voice, used for what the AI generates. One exception, on purpose: the "assumed" tag is
amber (feedback/warning), because it asks the person to check a value — a call to confirm, not the AI's voice.

## Glass & elevation

Three effect styles — `glass/thin`, `glass/regular`, `glass/thick` — each a background blur plus an
inset highlight and a drop shadow, all three colours of which are themselves tokens
(`color/glass/highlight`, `color/glass/shadow`). `glass/thin` is what ParameterChip uses for its
Assumed and Missing states.

<GlassSamples />

With **Reduce Transparency** on, every glass fill falls back to the solid `color/surface/raised` and
the blur drops to `0` — generated once, in `tokens.css`'s `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)`
block, so no component codes the fallback itself.
